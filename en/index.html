Minecraft VS Zombies 2 (MVZ2) is a fan-made game created solely by Cuerzor58, combining elements from Minecraft, Plants vs. Zombies, and the Touhou Project series. This site provides official links, download links, and official group links for various original and modified versions of MVZ2.

Footnotes

* Mobile Ports

In March 2025, 沐白_official created mobile ports of the Extra Mod, Expand (GMS) Mod, and v0.14.0 project (GM2.3), which contained numerous bugs. He is no longer involved in MVZ2 porting.
After June 2025, 秋冥散雨_GenOuka took over the mobile port updates for the v0.15.2+ Mod and began porting work for all other mods. Now almost all GMS mods have corresponding mobile ports.
These ported versions can be downloaded from 秋冥散雨's QQ Group (1058495844), 附魔Fumo_'s QQ Group (159464768), the respective mod's official QQ Group (see table), or the Discord Community (see table) (generally available in all three groups; if the mod is in closed beta, only the official group will have it).
